---

Events are listed earliest-first. Never edit a previously-logged Event in
place — a correction appends a new `## ... — Update <date>` block with an
`**Amends**:` link back to the original heading instead. See
`references/event-format.md` in the `llm-wiki` plugin's `log` skill for the
full field rules and the cross-wiki `[[label/slug]]` link convention.

## HH:MM — Action What

**Action**: What happened, in a few words.
**What**: [[label/slug]] — or plain text with an unresolved marker if no confident match exists yet.
**When**: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM+00:00
**Where**: Channel or medium (Slack #channel, Teams meeting, Zoom call, in person, ...).
**Who**: [[label/slug]], [[label/slug]]
**Why**: Rationale for the decision or action.
**Related events**: [[YYYY-MM-DD#Original Heading]]
**Next steps**:
- [ ] [[label/slug]] to do X by YYYY-MM-DD
